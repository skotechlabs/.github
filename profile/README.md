## About SKO TechLabs

**SKO TechLabs** is a software development company from Dhaka, Bangladesh, creating practical, scalable and user-friendly digital products. We work closely with founders and operations teams to turn ideas and operational challenges into reliable technology, and we stay on as a long-term technology partner after launch.

We work with startups, small and medium businesses and enterprise teams across **retail, healthcare, education, logistics and hospitality**.
